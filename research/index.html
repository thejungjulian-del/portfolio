Method

Three rules every result has to pass
Written down before the test, not after
.01 Split

Freeze the rules first.

Before I open the test data, the rules and the pass mark are written down and don’t move. 260 trading rules went through training, validation and an untouched final period. One survived. Each holdout file can only be used once.

Fig. 02 — rules in, survivors out
.02 Fill

Pay the real price.

Buy at the ask, sell at the bid, pay the fee, and only count a resting order as filled when the market trades through it. That one change turned my Polymarket bot’s $750 a month into zero.

Fig. 03 — the round trip pays the spread
.03 Control

Beat random dates.

Every event result gets compared with the same test on random dates. On Fed days a t of 2.41 looked great until 14.4% of random date sets matched it. Volatility, at t = 3.87, cleared the bar. Direction never did.

Research notes

Shorter questions, measured answers
Public APIs and published data
AI benchmark markets
The edge was reading which leaderboard settles the market. The same model scored anywhere from 44% to 64% across boards, and only Scale’s official board counted.
Hyperliquid
Built a SQLite collector for HIP-4 markets, which record forward because the API drops history on delisting. Measured the HLP vault falling from +101% a year in 2023 to +8% so far in 2026.
Crypto trend rules
A BTC 20-day trend rule fell from Sharpe 1.52 to 0.57 out-of-sample, barely above holding (0.48). The 200-day filter from the overnight study did carry over: BTC 0.98 → 1.15.
Futures signals
Signal Deck: the Claude API with live web search writing MES/ES futures setups to a Discord channel. Shelved before it went live.
Copy-trading
Top Polymarket wallets make their money by posting orders, not by picking winners, so copying their picks doesn’t copy the edge.
Memecoins
Published on-chain data: buying at pump.fun graduation averaged −60.7% within an hour across 41k tokens. The roles that reliably profit are insiders and same-block snipers.
Capital math
At the best edge that held (about 21% a year), $100 a trading day takes roughly $118k. Every asset class I tested gave the same answer: the limiting factor is capital.
Quant factors
The factors that last (momentum, value, low risk) work across many stocks over weeks to months. So Formula Lab ranks a basket instead of guessing one stock tomorrow.
