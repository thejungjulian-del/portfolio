About

Haas School of Business
UC Berkeley

Julian Jung

I’m a business student at UC Berkeley’s Haas School, in the Global Management Program. I like making the whole thing: the idea, the design, the code, the server it runs on, and the brand and film around it.

Right now that’s Creator Exchange, a play-money stock market for creators, and Redline, the campus car club, where I run design and web as vice president.

How I work

  • Every product here has a working demo in the dashboard, running on sample data.
  • Numbers on this page are checked against each project’s own data before they go up.
  • AI is part of most builds (Claude, Gemini and local models), and AI-generated people are labeled.
